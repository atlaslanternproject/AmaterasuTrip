Delete a trip

Amaterasu Trip allows you to permanently delete a trip while keeping your account active.

To delete a trip:

  1. Open Amaterasu Trip.
  2. Open the trip you want to delete.
  3. Go to Trip settings .
  4. Open Trip management .
  5. Select Delete trip .
  6. Enter the exact trip name to confirm deletion.

Deleting the trip permanently removes the relevant trip data managed by Amaterasu Trip.

Request deletion of other data

You may also request deletion of specific personal data while keeping your Amaterasu Trip account active.

Where possible, send the request from the same email address associated with your account.

Clearly specify which data you would like deleted.

Do not send passwords, access codes, identity documents or other credentials.

Request data deletion

Files in personal cloud storage

Deleting data or a trip from Amaterasu Trip does not automatically delete files stored in your personal cloud storage.

For example:

  • Google Drive
  • Microsoft OneDrive
  • Dropbox

These files remain under the control of your account with the relevant cloud provider and must be deleted directly through that service.
