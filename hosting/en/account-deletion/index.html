Account deletion

You can use this page to request the permanent deletion of your Amaterasu Trip account and the personal data associated with it.

Versione italiana

Delete your account in the app

If you can still access Amaterasu Trip, you can start the deletion process directly from the app.

  1. Open Amaterasu Trip.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open Account.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Complete the confirmation requested by the app.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, cannot sign in, or prefer to use an external method, you can request account deletion by email.

Where possible, send the request from the same email address associated with your Amaterasu Trip account so that we can identify the correct account.

Do not send passwords, access codes, identity documents or other credentials.

Request deletion by email

Data that is deleted

When account deletion is completed, Amaterasu Trip deletes the personal data associated with the account that is managed by the service, including, where applicable:

  • the Amaterasu Trip authentication account;
  • profile data associated with the account;
  • personal trip-profile data;
  • the user's associations with trips and groups;
  • preferences and technical references associated with the account;
  • other personal data directly linked to the Amaterasu Trip account.

Files stored with external cloud providers

Deleting your Amaterasu Trip account does not automatically delete files stored in your own personal cloud storage, such as Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive or Dropbox.

Those files remain under the control of your account with the relevant cloud provider. If you want to delete them, you must do so directly through that provider.

Processing time and retention

Requests submitted by email are processed as soon as reasonably possible and normally within 30 days, after sufficient verification that the request relates to the relevant account.

No additional retention period is planned for deleted application data once account deletion has been completed.

If specific information must be retained for legal, security or abuse-prevention obligations, only the minimum necessary information will be retained and only for the period required by the applicable obligation.
